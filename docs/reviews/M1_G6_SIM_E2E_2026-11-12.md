Status: SELF_TEST by the author of the g6 contract (Claude). It is NOT an independent QA and NOT an acceptance of g6.
The new edition is SIMULATED and fictional: no university published it. Reproduce: `python scripts/g6_sim_e2e.py <disposable-migrated-copy.sqlite> --evidence-id EV-T27-01-49cb4d` (prints JSON; exit 0 only when all 15 checks hold).

## Target and method

- Data: a COPY of the pinned real v06 file (SHA-256 `a8e48b5b...539578`, checked before and after: unchanged). The migration chain 0002..0014 (13 migrations) was applied to the copy with `additive_schema.apply(copy, confirm_disposable_copy=True)`. The real file was only read to copy it.
- Product code used, unchanged: `src/versioning/series.py` (series, persistence, `verify_series_against_rows`, `record_revision_impact`), `diff.py`, `impact.py`, `snapshot.py`, and the 0014 tables/triggers.
- OLD edition: source document `49cb4d7d...` (Tsukuba AC admission guide 2027, 15 pages in `source_pages`), its evidence (`facts` + `evidence_revisions`: 55 evidence records over 4 sources) and the 6 head articles (`article_heads` -> `revised_articles`).
- SIMULATED NEW edition: page 5 raw text, the quote of EV-T27-01 (the quota cell of the 知識情報・図書館学類 row) with its number `5` replaced by `6` (deterministic: first digit run of the quote + 1; the quote occurs exactly once on the page), plus one appended label page ("SIMULATED / 架空の版 ... 大学の公式発表ではありません"). The series title also says `[SIMULATED NEW EDITION - FICTIONAL, NOT AN OFFICIAL PUBLICATION]`. 0014 has no metadata column, so the label lives in the stored text and the series title.
- Series: `SIMULATED-g6-e2e-49cb4d7d`; rev1 = the 15 stored pages joined by a page-break marker (17,125 chars), rev2 = the simulated text (17,222 chars). Both persisted (2 rows); `verify_series_lineage` and `verify_series_against_rows` both pass.

## Limits (read before relying on this)

- SIMULATED new edition: the change is mechanical and chosen by the author; no real revised edition of any document was used, so this says nothing about real-world revision detection quality (re-typeset PDFs, moved tables, reworded clauses).
- SELF_TEST by the author of the contract; no independent QA, no acceptance of g6 is claimed.
- No real article corpus: the 6 articles are the head drafts stored in the pinned file (unpublished, `publish_allowed` 0); no published article was examined.
- The full-article-QA FAIL (13 articles) is NOT addressed by this work.
- The flagging is limited to evidence that carries a stored quote and whose source has stored pages; `EV-U-*` evidence stays `unassessed`. Same-page neighbours are reported as `review_same_page`, not as candidates.
- The series stores page text of the stored extraction (which includes junk control characters on one page); the series hash is the hash of that joined text, not the PDF's SHA-256 (the PDF hash is kept in the snapshot link `supersedes`).
- Nothing was written to the real database; nothing was published; Work-owned code was not touched.
